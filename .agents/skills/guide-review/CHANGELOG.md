## 1.0.0 (2026-08-01)

Initial public release.

### Added
- Six-agent review panel: Series Editor, Non-Technical Proxy, Practitioner Proxy, Consistency Auditor, Diagram Reviewer, Claims Auditor.
- Five workflows: single-chapter review (quick), full-series audit (thorough), consistency check (targeted), diagram audit (targeted), claims audit (targeted).
- Quality gate checklist.
- Designed to be portable — configurable for any multi-chapter guide or book via the "Configure for your work" section.
